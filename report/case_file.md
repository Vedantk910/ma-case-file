# M&A Screening Case File — Project P3
### E-commerce / Logistics Portfolio | One-Page Executive Summary

---

## Governing conclusion

**Buy only where the entry multiple is 8x EBITDA or below and at least 50% of
stated synergy is contractually secured; that is the entire accretive region of
the deal set — every screen above it is dilutive in year one and the LBO math
does not close.** The single most important number in this file is **+3.2%
year-1 EPS accretion at a 6x entry with full (100%) synergy capture**, and the
whole 6x–12x × 0–100%-capture grid confirms that **only the cheapest,
best-captured deals are accretive** (12x entry is dilutive at every capture
level).

---

## Why (quantified)

- **Accretion / dilution (100-unit EPS).** The 7 × 4 sweep spans **+3.2%
  (6x, full capture) down to −0.6% (12x, no capture)**. The break-even entry
  multiple for year-1 EPS neutrality is roughly **8x–9x** before synergies;
  synergy capture is worth up to ~2.0 pts of EPS, the largest single lever
  next to the entry multiple.

- **DCF discount-rate sensitivity.** A 100-unit EBITDA target values
  **$3,123 → $1,138 as WACC moves 6% → 12%** — a **~174% spread**, i.e. the
  implied multiple moves from **~31x to ~11x**. The terminal value is 65–85%
  of enterprise value, so the screen is hostage to the discount-rate
  assumption; this is why the entry multiple must be locked before the DCF
  debate.

- **LBO-lite roll-up IRR (5-yr hold, 4% EBITDA CAGR, bisection IRR).** At
  **4x leverage**, a 6x entry / 12x exit prints **~39.6% IRR**, while a 12x
  entry / 6x exit is **−16.2%** — the entry multiple is the IRR pivot. Even
  at 4x, any entry above ~9x only clears a sponsor hurdle on a rising-multiple
  exit.

- **Deal book (12 public deals, 2015–2025).** Average announced value
  **$5.16B**, ranging **$0.33B (JD–Debon) to $16B (Walmart–Flipkart)**.
  Most are strategic / revenue-priced; a clean public EBITDA multiple is
  **not** available for any of the 12, so the screen's multiples are the
  decision inputs, not the historical deal values.

---

## What we would ask the client (5 discovery questions)

1. **Entry-multiple ceiling:** At what EV/EBITDA does your committee stop
   being accretive even at 100% capture — and how much of that is tolerance
   vs. a hard cap?
2. **Synergy capture & contractability:** What share of stated annual synergy
   do you hold to a contractual / 8-K-committed basis in year 1, and who
   signs it?
3. **Discount-rate policy:** What WACC band does the committee use for
   valuation, and is the terminal value a material share of EV here — can
   we de-weight it so the multiple, not the rate, drives the range?
4. **Leverage & IRR hurdle:** At what debt/EBITDA (3x/4x/5x) does the roll-up
   clear your target IRR, and is exit at a flat or expanding multiple assumed?
5. **Sourcing bar:** Given e-commerce/logistics targets are rarely
   EBITDA-transparent, what diligence replaces a public multiple (unit economics,
   revenue quality, retention) before a deal reaches the screen?
